About GreenPath

Working Together for a Cleaner Planet

GreenPath is an environmental website that encourages people to help keep their communities clean.

Users can record the bottles, wrappers, and cans they collect. Each cleanup may seem small, but together these actions can make a meaningful difference.

Our Mission

Our mission is to inspire people to take simple actions that protect the environment and improve their communities.

Why It Matters

Removing litter helps protect wildlife, reduce pollution, and create cleaner and safer neighborhoods.

How It Works

Collect litter, choose the correct logging page, enter your name and amount, and save your cleanup result.
